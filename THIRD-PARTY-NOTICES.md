## FFmpeg (GPL v3)

`ffmpeg.exe` (Windows) and `ffmpeg` (inside `Clipiccolo.app/Contents/MacOS`) are separate programs. Clipiccolo
starts them as a child process and talks to them only through command-line arguments and pipes; they are not
linked into Clipiccolo.

- Windows build: FFmpeg 8.0 "essentials" by [gyan.dev](https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/), configured with
  `--enable-gpl --enable-version3`. Run `ffmpeg.exe -version` for the full configuration and library list.
- Mac build: FFmpeg 9.0.2 for macOS/arm64 by [Martin Riedl](https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/), configured with
  `--enable-gpl --enable-version3` (libx264, x265, libvpx, aom, dav1d, Opus, LAME and others; nothing from the
  "nonfree" set). Run `Clipiccolo.app/Contents/MacOS/ffmpeg -version` for the full list.
- License: GNU General Public License v3. The full text ships next to it as `FFMPEG-LICENSE.txt`
  (Mac: `Clipiccolo.app/Contents/Resources/FFMPEG-LICENSE.txt`).
- Copyright (c) 2000-2026 the FFmpeg developers, plus the authors of the libraries in the builds (x264, x265,
  libvpx, libaom, LAME, Opus and others).
- **Source code:** every [GitHub release](https://github.com/hubertmk/clipiccolo/releases) that ships an ffmpeg
  binary also carries the matching FFmpeg source archive(s). Upstream sources are at
  <https://ffmpeg.org/download.html>, <https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/> and <https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/>.
  If an archive is missing, open an issue and it will be provided, for at least three years after the release.
