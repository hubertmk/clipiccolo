# Surface brief: site/index.html (and site/privacy/index.html)

Scope: the public marketing site for Clipiccolo, one long scroll with anchor navigation plus a privacy subpage in the same shell. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: anyone on Windows with a video too big to send, about to paste it somewhere. Job: understand in seconds that Clipiccolo turns the video on their clipboard into a small MP4 back on their clipboard, then download it. Action: Download for Windows (GitHub releases). Proof: the product itself at work (the loop), the real preset table, real install facts from README.md, honest notes about SmartScreen. Constraints: no testimonials, counts, benchmarks, pricing or platform promises; one Italian word per screen; pistachio under 5% and only ever the small thing of a pair.

Structure (confirmed with the user 2026-11-13): Dettino's shell. Links: GitHub and buymeacoffee.com/hubertmk, no email. Hero may be reworked.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The clipboard in both directions is the whole story: copy, switch, paste. The page refuses the converter-site template (feature grid of icon cards, a comparison table, a screenshot carousel) and instead walks the one round trip, then answers the questions a careful person asks before installing an unsigned exe.

OWN-WORLD: Crema desk, milk sheets, espresso ink, hairlines for structure, no shadows except the loop card and toast. Bricolage Grotesque for everything said, DM Mono with tabular figures for everything measured (The Receipt Rule). Pistachio appears only as the small dot, the progress fill and the saving line. Controls are 7px rectangles; one espresso button per view (The Counter Rule), echoed in the sticky nav.

STORY: "My video is on the clipboard. I switch to Clipiccolo. I switch back and paste." The visitor believes it because they watch it happen in the loop, then read the facts in receipt type, and learn nothing leaves their PC. They download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header (lockup left; How it works, Quality, Privacy, FAQ; GitHub icon; espresso Download button). Below, the family frame: headline over two lines ending in the pistachio dot, lede, Download for Windows with mono fine print on the left; the product loop on the right in a dashed drop zone. 1.1fr/1fr grid, stacks under 760px.

FORM: Long-scroll product page in the HuKe family frame: hero, the round trip in three stops (Copy / Switch / Paste) as a receipt-style timeline with the clipboard drawn at each stop, the three presets as one segmented receipt table, in/out facts as two columns of plain sentences, privacy as a bulleted statement, the small things as a hairline-divided list, one Windows download card with a spec list, a Q&A FAQ, one-line footer. Position 1 of 1: extension of an existing surface. seed: none (pinned) "like dettino.com", "Dettino's structure".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
